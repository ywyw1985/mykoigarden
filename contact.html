Best ways to use the contact page

For koi health questions, include water test numbers, pond size, filtration type, water temperature, recent changes, and clear photos if available. For local koi sale questions, mention the fish you are interested in and whether you can pick up in South Florida.

Current contact method: use the community Q&A page for public questions and the local sale inquiry link for koi-for-sale questions. A dedicated email/contact form can be added next when you decide what inbox you want to use.

Contact topics

  • Questions about koi care, water quality, pond filtration, feeding, or variety identification.
  • South Florida local koi pickup inquiries.
  • Corrections, missing sources, unclear wording, or content suggestions.
  • Image credit, permission, or removal requests.
  • Collaboration with koi clubs, pond builders, breeders, or hobby communities.

Before asking health questions

Please test ammonia, nitrite, nitrate, pH, KH if possible, water temperature, and dissolved oxygen if you have a meter. Many apparent disease problems begin with water quality, low oxygen, crowding, or recent stress.
