Local pickup area

This page is intended for South Florida buyers within roughly a one-hour local pickup range. Nearby city examples include Davie, Weston, Southwest Ranches, Pembroke Pines, Cooper City, Fort Lauderdale, Plantation, Sunrise, Miramar, Hollywood, Miami Lakes, Boca Raton, and Coral Springs. ZIP code 33331 is only an approximate pickup-area hint; exact pickup details should be shared privately after buyer screening.

Buyer preparation

Before pickupWhy it matters
Bring a clean transport tub, koi bag, or covered container.Koi should not be moved in small buckets that bend the body or damage fins.
Use dechlorinated water and temperature awareness.Sudden water-quality or temperature changes can stress fish during transport.
Bring battery aeration for longer drives.Warm South Florida weather can reduce dissolved oxygen quickly.
Prepare a quarantine tank or separate pond area at home.New koi should be observed before joining an established pond.

Health and sale notes

  • Fish are sold as local pond fish, not certified show koi.
  • Buyer should inspect fish photos, video, approximate size, body condition, and behavior before purchase.
  • Size is an estimate only. Koi are difficult to measure precisely in water, and final judgment should be based on the actual fish at pickup.
  • No shipping is offered because koi transport requires proper oxygen, temperature control, and handling.
  • All sales should be final after pickup unless otherwise agreed in writing.
