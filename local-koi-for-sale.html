Buyer preparation

Before pickupWhy it matters
Bring a clean transport tub, koi bag, or covered container.Koi should not be moved in small buckets that bend the body or damage fins.
Use dechlorinated water and temperature awareness.Sudden water-quality or temperature changes can stress fish during transport.
Bring battery aeration for longer drives.Warm South Florida weather can reduce dissolved oxygen quickly.
Prepare a quarantine tank or separate pond area at home.New koi should be observed before joining an established pond.
