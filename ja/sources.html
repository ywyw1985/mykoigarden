研究ソース

  • 全日本錦鯉振興会：各種 - variety names and Nishikigoi classification reference.

  • 小千谷市：錦鯉 - regional history and Niigata/Ojiya cultural context.
  • 山古志地域情報 - Niigata mountain-village context connected with Nishikigoi culture.
  • 全日本愛隣会：錦鯉の品種 - show appreciation groups and major variety categories.
  • キョーリンヒカリ：錦鯉品種ガイド - Japanese-language variety reference and koi education context.
  • 松田養鯉場：品種紹介 - Japanese-language descriptions of major varieties.
  • FAO の水産養殖マニュアル: コイの種苗生産の背景 - general carp breeding, egg, fry, and nursery-management background.
  • メルク獣医マニュアル: 魚の日常的な健康管理 - health management focus on water quality, nutrition, sanitation, and quarantine.
  • メルク獣医マニュアル: 魚の障害と病気 - fish disease context, stress, water quality, parasites, and professional treatment guidance.
  • メルク獣医マニュアル: 日常的な水質分析の正常基準範囲 - routine fish water-quality ranges used as a professional reference point.
  • フロリダ大学 IFAS: 水系中のアンモニア - ammonia chemistry, toxicity, pH, and temperature context for fish systems.
  • 南部地域水産養殖センター: 養殖池の亜硝酸塩 - nitrite toxicity and management guidance for pond fish.
  • ミシシッピ州立大学の拡張機能: pH とアルカリ度 - alkalinity, hardness, and pH stability background for pond management.
  • FDA: 観賞魚の Ich 治療 - regulatory update on Ich treatment availability for ornamental fish.
  • Amazon アソシエイト: アフィリエイト開示ヘルプ - disclosure requirements for affiliate links.
  • FTC 承認ガイドに関するよくある質問 - material connection and affiliate disclosure guidance.
  • Baidu Baike: 蝴蝶鲤 - Chinese-language overview of butterfly koi naming and appearance.
  • Baidu Baike: 锦鲤 - Chinese-language overview of koi/carp cultural and historical context.
  • 百度京岩水族館の飼育記事 - Chinese hobbyist context on tank-raised ornamental fish care, water changes, and filtration habits.
