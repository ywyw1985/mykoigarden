  • Amazon Associates: affiliate disclosure help - disclosure requirements for affiliate links.

  • Baidu Baike: 蝴蝶鲤 - Chinese-language overview of butterfly koi naming and appearance.

  • Baidu Jingyan aquarium keeping articles - Chinese hobbyist context on tank-raised ornamental fish care, water changes, and filtration habits.
