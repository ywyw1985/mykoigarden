Beyond Gosanke, the koi world includes old-line varieties, metallic groups, single-color pond favorites, and special scale types.

Major groups to learn next

  • Group
  • Identification cue
  • Quality cue
  • Shusui
  • Doitsu Asagi relative with mirror scales along the back.
  • Neat dorsal scale line, clean head, controlled red on sides.
  • Goromo / Koromo
  • Kohaku-like red pattern with blue/dark edging over the red.
  • Clean white ground and reticulation that enhances rather than dirties the beni.
  • Goshiki
  • Five-color impression, often red pattern over darker reticulated ground.
  • Clear red pattern, balanced dark ground, no muddy confusion.
  • Kujaku
  • Metallic koi with reticulation and orange/red pattern.
  • Strong metallic sheen plus orderly reticulation.
  • Chagoi / Soragoi / Ochiba
  • Single-color or autumn-leaf pond favorites.
  • Body, scale neatness, calm behavior, and even color.
  • Gin Rin / Doitsu
  • Cross-cutting scale traits, not always standalone varieties.
  • Even sparkle for Gin Rin; neat scale rows for Doitsu.
