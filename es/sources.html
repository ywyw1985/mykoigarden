Notas editoriales

Fuentes y créditos de imágenes.

My Koi Garden utiliza referencias profesionales de koi para estructura y terminología, evitando al mismo tiempo la copia sin licencia de fotografías de criadores o asociaciones.

Fuentes de investigación

  • Asociación de Promoción de Nishikigoi de todo Japón: Variedad - variety names and Nishikigoi classification reference.
  • Asociación de Promoción de Nishikigoi de todo Japón: descripción general de Nishikigoi - modern Nishikigoi history and cultural context.
  • Ciudad de Ojiya: Nishikigoi - regional history and Niigata/Ojiya cultural context.
  • Información regional de Yamakoshi - Niigata mountain-village context connected with Nishikigoi culture.
  • Zen Nippon Airinkai: Variedades de Nishikigoi - show appreciation groups and major variety categories.
  • Kyorin Hikari: guía de variedades Nishikigoi - Japanese-language variety reference and koi education context.
  • Matsuda Koi Farm: introducción a la variedad - Japanese-language descriptions of major varieties.
  • Manual de acuicultura de la FAO: contexto de producción de semillas de carpa - general carp breeding, egg, fry, and nursery-management background.
  • Manual veterinario de Merck: cuidados sanitarios rutinarios de los peces - health management focus on water quality, nutrition, sanitation, and quarantine.
  • Manual veterinario de Merck: trastornos y enfermedades de los peces - fish disease context, stress, water quality, parasites, and professional treatment guidance.
  • Manual veterinario de Merck: rangos de referencia normales para análisis rutinarios de la calidad del agua - routine fish water-quality ranges used as a professional reference point.
  • IFAS de la Universidad de Florida: Amoníaco en sistemas acuáticos - ammonia chemistry, toxicity, pH, and temperature context for fish systems.
  • Centro Regional de Acuicultura Sur: Nitrito en estanques de peces - nitrite toxicity and management guidance for pond fish.
  • Extensión de la Universidad Estatal de Mississippi: pH y alcalinidad - alkalinity, hardness, and pH stability background for pond management.
  • FDA: tratamiento Ich para peces ornamentales - regulatory update on Ich treatment availability for ornamental fish.
  • Asociados de Amazon: ayuda para la divulgación de afiliados - disclosure requirements for affiliate links.
  • Preguntas frecuentes sobre las guías de respaldo de la FTC - material connection and affiliate disclosure guidance.
  • Baidu Baike: 蝴蝶鲤 - Chinese-language overview of butterfly koi naming and appearance.
  • Baidu Baike: 锦鲤 - Chinese-language overview of koi/carp cultural and historical context.
  • Artículos de mantenimiento del acuario Baidu Jingyan - Chinese hobbyist context on tank-raised ornamental fish care, water changes, and filtration habits.
  • FAO: Fish culture in undrainable ponds, nutrition section - carp nutrition, supplementary feeding, protein requirement factors, and pond-culture feeding context.
  • FAO: The nutrition and feeding of farmed fish and shrimp - complete and supplementary diet context, protein level interpretation, and pond-feeding limitations.
  • SRAC Publication No. 5003: Principles of Fish Nutrition - fish nutrition principles, digestible energy, protein, ingredient quality, and feeding-practice context.
  • Hikari Wheat-Germ Formula information - koi wheat-germ feeding context and guidance to adjust feeding by water temperature and fish activity.
  • Hikari Sales USA: Feeding coldwater koi basics - coldwater koi feeding, small portions, and seasonal feeding caution.
  • Fiverkoi: What is a koi pond drum filter? - rotary drum filter / RDF visual reference for koi pond mechanical filtration.
  • Surrey Koi: K1 moving bed filter - moving bed biológical filtration visual reference.
  • Ricky Stoddart: Using shower filters in your koi pond - shower and trickle filtration visual reference.
  • The Pond Guy: PowerUV UV Clarifier - UV clarifier visual reference for green-water control.
  • University of Florida IFAS: Koi Herpesvirus Disease - KHV, quarantine, biosecurity, and laboratory-testing context.
  • Merck Veterinary Manual: Viral Diseases of Fish - viral disease context for aquarium and pond fish.
