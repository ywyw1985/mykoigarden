Koi Care

Koi keeping starts with stable water.

Learn the routines that make koi ponds calmer: testing, feeding, filtration, observation, and seasonal adjustments.

RoutineSuggested frequencyWhat to record
Observe fishDailyAppetite, breathing, fin position, isolation, flashing, surface behavior.
Check temperatureDaily during feeding seasonWater temperature before changing food amount.
Test ammonia/nitrite/pHWeekly in stable ponds; more often after changesActual numbers, not just "good" or "bad".
Clean mechanical filterAs waste accumulatesDate, amount of sludge, flow improvement.
Review stocking and feedingMonthly in warm seasonFish growth, food volume, nitrate trend, oxygen stress signs.
