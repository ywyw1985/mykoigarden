How the daily AI workflow should work

  1. Collect public source updates from association pages, show calendars, breeder/dealer announcements, RSS feeds, and YouTube channel feeds.
  2. Classify each item as show, auction, breeder update, video, market note, or learning resource.
  3. Generate a short original summary with source attribution and a direct link.
  4. Flag date-sensitive or commercial claims for owner review before publishing.
  5. Publish approved summaries to this page and add topic-specific videos to relevant care, pond, or variety pages only when they fit naturally.
