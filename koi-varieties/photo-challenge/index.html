Can you identify these koi?

Five real koi photos. Choose your answer, then open the explanation to see which marking gives it away.

12345
Question 1

Which variety has this red-and-white pattern?

Reveal the answer

Kohaku. The red patches sit on white skin. The pale band between them is a break in the red pattern.

Question 2

Which variety matches the black pattern on this fish?

Reveal the answer

Showa. Trace the broad black markings across the head and down the flank. The head marking and the wraparound black make this a useful example to compare with Sanke.

Question 3

Of the two fish in the center, which is the Goshiki?

Reveal the answer

The right fish is Goshiki. Notice the gray netlike scales between the red markings. The Sanke on the left has distinct black patches on its white-and-red body.

Question 4

What is the name of this red head pattern?

Reveal the answer

Tancho. This Tancho Kohaku has a single red cap and a white body. A Maruten Kohaku also carries red markings behind the head.

Question 5

Which variety has this row of blue-gray scales?

Reveal the answer

Shusui. Follow the line of large, dark blue-gray scales along the spine. The smooth-looking flanks carry red, while an Asagi shows a finer net of scales across its back.
