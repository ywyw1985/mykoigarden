Variety Guide

Ogon

Ogon are single-color metallic koi where body, shine, and even color take center stage.

Identity

Ogon are appreciated for metallic luster and clean simplicity. Because there is no complex pattern to distract the eye, body shape and even shine are especially important.

Beginner notes

  • Look for strong, even metallic sheen from head through tail.
  • Color should look consistent, not patchy or dull.
  • A good Ogon can be very visible and satisfying in a garden pond.
