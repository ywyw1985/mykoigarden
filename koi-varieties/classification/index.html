How Japan classifies koi varieties

The 22 JAS variety entries · What changed in 2026? · Pictures and further reading

How many koi varieties are there?

The All Japan Nishikigoi Promotion Association describes over 150 varieties in its Japanese introduction. Its English FAQ says over 140 and notes that new varieties appear almost every year. Both are lower bounds. An exact world total would require a single rule for counting every named form.

Show classes combine named forms. The promotion association’s FAQ describes 22 classes, while Zen Nippon Airinkai lists 17 basic classes and additional classes for male koi. Use the entry rules of the particular show.

Japanese introduction · English FAQ · ZNA show classes

What changed in 2026?

On 30 September 2026, the Japanese Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries revised JAS 0020. The variety section now has 22 numbered entries, up from 21. Beni Kikokuryu illustrates the new metallic Hikari-kawarigoi entry (2008). Ochiba Shigure remains an example of Kawarigoi, now 2010. The English PDF of the 2022 edition uses the old numbering.
