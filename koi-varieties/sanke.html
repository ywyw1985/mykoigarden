Variety Guide

Taisho Sanke

Sanke is a white koi with red pattern and black accents, often giving a refined and elegant impression.

Identity

Sanke builds on the red-and-white foundation of Kohaku by adding black markings. A helpful beginner idea is that the white base should still feel important, with black used as accent rather than heavy background.

Beginner notes

  • Look for a clean white base and balanced red pattern.
  • Black markings should support the overall impression.
  • Sanke usually does not have heavy black on the head like Showa can.
