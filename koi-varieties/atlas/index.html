How many koi varieties are there?

The All Japan Nishikigoi Promotion Association describes more than 150 varieties. Its English FAQ gives a lower bound of 140 and describes 22 show classes.

A show class can contain several named forms. Traits can also occur across varieties. Ginrin, for example, describes the shine of individual scales. The atlas labels these entries separately.

Scales, markings & fin forms

Trait

Doitsu

ドイツ鯉

Much of the skin is exposed. Check for rows of large scales left along the spine or sides. This scale type occurs in many varieties.

Also called Doitsugoi, German-scaled, 德系少鳞, ドイツ

Read the guideReference · JNPA

Trait

Hirenaga (longfin koi)

Hirenaga · ヒレナガニシキゴイ

Watch the fish turn. Its long pectoral fins and tail trail behind the body. The fin shape can accompany several different color patterns.

Also called Hirenaga Nishikigoi, butterfly koi, longfin, 蝴蝶鲤, 鰭長錦鯉, koi mariposa

Read the guideReference · pref.saitama.lg.jp

Trait

Matsuba

松葉紋

The dark centers of neighboring scales repeat like the scales of a pinecone. The effect occurs on metallic and nonmetallic fish.

Also called Matsubamon, Matsuba-mon, 松叶

Read the guideReference · ceylonkoisociety.com

Trait

Wagoi

和鯉

Regular scales cover nearly the entire body. Follow a row from the back down the flank to distinguish it from Doitsu scaling.

Also called Fully scaled, 和鲤
