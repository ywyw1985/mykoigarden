Pond Guide

The pond is the koi's real home.

Plan the system around water quality, oxygen, circulation, filtration, shade, and maintenance access.

Filtration is the heart of the system

Mechanical filtration removes solids. Biological filtration supports bacteria that process waste. A pond can look beautiful and still be unstable if the filter is undersized or neglected.

Movement and oxygen

Water movement helps distribute oxygen and move debris toward filtration. Aeration becomes especially important in warm weather, crowded ponds, and during heavy feeding.

Shade and plants

Shade can reduce temperature stress and algae pressure. Plants can soften the garden edge, but koi may disturb roots, so plant placement should be practical.

Maintenance access

A pond that is easy to clean is more likely to stay healthy. Leave space to reach filters, pumps, valves, and netting points without turning every task into a project.
