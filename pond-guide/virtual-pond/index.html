The virtual koi pond

Four koi are swimming in this illustrated pond. Try following one by its markings. Scatter a few virtual pellets and see if you can pick out the same fish when the group gathers.

How to find your fish again

Look at where the colors fall on each illustrated fish. The same colors can form very different patterns on real koi; each name below opens the photographic guide.

Kohaku
Follow the red patches along the white back. In this pond the Kohaku has three red islands and no black marks.
Taisho Sanke
Find the small black markings among the larger red patches. The Sanke here is the only model with all three colors.
Tancho Kohaku
Watch the single red circle near the nose. The rest of this model’s white back is clear, which makes it easy to find after a turn.
Yamabuki Ogon
Track the solid golden body as it changes direction. Here the sheen moves with the fish while its single-color pattern stays the same.

Koi outlines and colors are adapted from Koi Pond Garden by Sourany Phomhome (MIT license). The swimming and feeding are a play simulation. Variety names follow the Japanese Agricultural Standard for Nishikigoi.
