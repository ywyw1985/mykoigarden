Construction

Pond materials and build methods

Choose pond materials as a complete system: the supporting structure, waterproof layer and cleanable finish must work with the drains, filters and future maintenance. A material name alone does not establish suitability for koi.

EPDM liner ponds

EPDM is a flexible rubber membrane; use a product documented for fish ponds and its specified joining system. Prepare a firm, smooth base, remove sharp objects and use the protective underlay specified for the site. Underlay protects the liner; it is not the waterproof layer.

If a specification says 45 mil, that means 0.045 inch, approximately 1.14 mm. It does not mean 45 mm. This is a unit conversion, not a universal minimum or a recommendation for every pond.

Concrete ponds

Plan the structure and waterproofing together, including joints and pipe penetrations. Follow the specified concrete preparation, curing and coating requirements. A surface that looks dry is not proof that the complete system is ready for filling or fish. Check water conditions during commissioning; do not apply a fixed curing period or an acid-treatment recipe to every pond.

Fiberglass lining and coatings

FRP lining and pond coatings have different preparation and application requirements. Confirm that the exact system is suitable for continuous immersion and fish ponds once fully cured. Touch-dry and fully cured are different stages; temperature and product instructions affect the wait. Use compatible primers, fillers and seals, especially around pipes.

Preformed pond shells

Choose by the actual operating volume, depth, shape and planned fish load, together with filtration and aeration. A small decorative shell is not automatically suitable for growing koi, but the category alone does not establish suitability either. Follow its support and backfilling instructions and check whether fittings can be installed as intended.

Block walls and raised ponds

A raised pond also needs a design for the full water load, lateral pressure and site conditions. Have the walls, foundation and any viewing panel designed for that installation. A waterproof coating does not replace a load-bearing structure. Include safe edges and access for inspection.

Stone finishes and pond cleaning

Stone is a finish, not a substitute for a waterproof system. Gaps can collect sediment; plan how it will be removed without damaging the liner. Compare access for inspection, drains and cleaning before choosing a covered or exposed pond floor.

Details to decide before construction

  • Decide bottom drains, skimmers, returns, overflows and service access before closing the structure.
  • Match each pipe seal, flange and adhesive to the actual liner or coating system.
  • Keep surface runoff out of the pond and plan where overflow will go.
  • Have outdoor electrical work and protective devices specified for local requirements.
  • Record product details and complete leak, circulation and water checks before proceeding to biological startup.
