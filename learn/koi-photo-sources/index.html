Reading a koi photograph

Which details can you see in a photograph, and which come from the keeper’s notes? Look at five fish photographed from above, then check their labels against guides in Japanese, Chinese and Dutch.

Two young koi from Dainichi

Both fish are tosai, koi in their first year. Begin at the head and follow the black markings toward the tail. Then check what the photographer recorded about each fish.

Three koi photographed at Konishi

Martin Kammerer photographed these individuals at Konishi Koi Farm in Hiroshima on 9 September 2024. Compare the colour of the body with the way individual scales catch the light.

Look up a name or feature

Start with the Japanese spelling, then compare how a regional association describes the same feature.

  • Japanese koi vocabulary standard (JAS 0020) — Find the official Japanese term and its romanization.
  • Niigata breeders’ variety list — Cross-check variety names against individual examples.
  • Taoyuan Koi Research Association — Look up 元黑 in the entry for Showa. The term refers to black at the base of a pectoral fin.
  • Guangdong Ocean University’s ornamental aquaculture course — The syllabus lists koi varieties and classification in lesson 4.2 and appreciation and grading in lesson 4.3.
  • Dutch koi association on Shusui — Compare the dorsal scale row with the scales along the sides.
  • Dutch koi association on Goshiki — Read how Dutch keepers describe the interplay of the ground and overlying pattern.
  • Niigata fisheries station on Kijiro — Compare yellow-and-white individuals identified by birth year.
