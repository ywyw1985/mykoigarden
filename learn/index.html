Four details to look for

Sanke and Showa

Both have red, white and black. Start with the ground color. Sanke has a white ground, while Showa carries red and white markings on a black ground.

Sanke · Showa

Bekko and Utsuri

On a Bekko, look for separate dark spots on a lighter body. On an Utsuri, follow the black ground through the pattern.

Bekko · Utsuri

Asagi and Shusui

An Asagi has a net-like scale pattern across the back. On a Shusui, the row of larger dorsal scales stands out against smoother skin.

Asagi · Shusui

Ogon and Gin Rin

Look at where the light falls. An Ogon has a metallic sheen across the body. Gin Rin describes reflective scales and can occur on a patterned koi.

Ogon · Gin Rin
