Where do Japan’s koi go?

Japan exported 378 tonnes of koi worth ¥9.97 billion in 2025. The United States and China together accounted for 50.3% of that value. How did the trade change as it grew?

Seven years of koi exports

From 2019 to 2025, export value grew to about 2.5 times its starting level. The weight shipped rose by about 38%. Switch measures to see where the two trends diverged.

How to read the figures

The series starts in 2019, when Japan began reporting koi separately. The earlier category included other ornamental fish and excluded goldfish. The figures are calendar-year export values in yen and shipment weights in metric tonnes. They give no count of fish or prices for individual koi.

My Koi Garden created these charts and tables from Japan’s Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries (MAFF) reports, compiled from Ministry of Finance trade statistics. The 2025 figures are from the revised annual release. Published figures and percentages are rounded separately.
